Goal Tycoon privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Goal Tycoon is made by Jake Scott ("we"). Contact: Jake.scott247@gmail.com.

What the game stores

Your game progress is saved on your device only. We do not run accounts or servers for the game and we do not sell personal data.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Before ads load we ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the UK and EEA) and, on iPhone, Apple's permission to track. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data from partner apps. You can change your choices in your device settings.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them; it receives an anonymous app user id and your purchase history. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

You can ask us about or delete any data linked to you by emailing Jake.scott247@gmail.com. Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress.
